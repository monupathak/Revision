# Artificial Intelligence

Revision notebooks covering classical AI concepts and problem-solving methods.

## Topics

- `01_search`: State-space search, uninformed search, informed search, and adversarial search
- `02_knowledge_representation`: Logic, rules, graphs, and structured knowledge
- `03_reasoning`: Inference, uncertainty, and decision-making
- `04_planning`: Goal-based planning and action sequences
- `05_projects`: Larger exercises that combine multiple AI concepts

Add notebooks in learning order and use numbered `snake_case` filenames.
